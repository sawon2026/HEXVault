# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the HEXVault community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

**Positive:** empathy, respect, constructive feedback, responsibility, community focus.

**Unacceptable:** sexualized language/imagery, trolling, harassment, doxxing, other unprofessional conduct.

## Enforcement

Report issues to **sawonislammd71@gmail.com**. Maintainers will review promptly and fairly.

This Code of Conduct applies in all community spaces and when representing the project publicly.
